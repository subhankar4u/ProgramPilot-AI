# ProgramPilot AI ### See delivery risks before they become business failures. ProgramPilot AI is an AI-powered enterprise program observability and early-warning platform built for Program Managers, Project Managers, PMO Leaders, Delivery Leaders, and Executives. It transforms fragmented program information into explainable risks, correlated warning signals, recommended decisions, and executive-ready insights. --- ## Hackathon Theme **Observability** Technical teams use observability platforms to monitor applications and infrastructure. However, Program Managers often depend on spreadsheets, meetings, RAID logs, and manually prepared status reports to understand program health. ProgramPilot AI brings observability to enterprise program execution. --- ## The Problem Program information is usually distributed across: - Meeting transcripts - RAID logs - Project plans - Status reports - Milestone trackers - Action logs - Stakeholder communications - Budget reports - Capacity plans - Cross-project dependencies By the time leaders recognize a serious delivery risk, the milestone may already be delayed. --- ## The Solution ProgramPilot AI converts fragmented project information into a real-time, explainable view of program health. It helps leaders understand: 1. What is changing? 2. Why is it changing? 3. What could happen next? 4. What action should be taken? Most project-management tools report what has already happened. ProgramPilot AI helps leaders anticipate what may happen next. --- ## Core Features ### Program Command Center A unified executive dashboard showing: - Program Health Score - Schedule confidence - Budget confidence - Dependency health - Team capacity - Active alerts - Upcoming milestones - Overdue actions ### Program Signal Stream Monitors signals from: - Meetings - RAID logs - Milestones - Actions - Dependencies - Budgets - Capacity reports - Stakeholder updates ### AI Program Analyzer Converts unstructured meeting notes and project updates into: - Risks - Issues - Decisions - Actions - Owners - Deadlines - Dependencies - Capacity constraints - Schedule warnings ### Early-Warning Engine Identifies emerging threats and presents: - Alert severity - Confidence score - Supporting evidence - Root-cause hypothesis - Potential business impact - Recommended intervention ### Signal Correlation Connects multiple weak signals that traditional reporting may examine separately. Example: - Vendor has not responded - Security approval is pending - Integration testing starts next week - No contingency owner exists ProgramPilot AI correlates these signals and identifies a material Release 3 delivery risk. ### Explainable Health Score The Program Health Score considers: - Critical risks - Delayed milestones - Overdue actions - Blocked dependencies - Budget variance - Capacity pressure - Delayed decisions - Stakeholder responsiveness Every score change is explained. The application does not present an unexplained AI-generated number. ### Program Trace View Follows a business outcome across its complete dependency chain: `Release 3 → Security Approval → Vendor Response → Integration Testing → User Acceptance Testing → Production Release` ### AI Decision Assistant Recommends: - Immediate action - Responsible owner - Suggested deadline - Expected impact - Confidence level - Supporting evidence - Alternative response ### Executive Report Generator Creates a decision-ready steering update containing: - Overall program health - Material changes - Critical risks - Delayed milestones - Leadership decisions required - Recommended actions - Next seven-day priorities ### Program Intelligence Chat Users can ask: - Why did program health decline? - What is threatening Release 3? - Which dependency requires escalation? - What changed this week? - What decision must leadership make? - What should I prioritize today? ### Business Impact Dashboard Displays prototype estimates for: - Reporting time saved - Risks detected early - Potential delays avoided - Overdue actions identified - Decisions accelerated - Estimated cost exposure --- ## Judge Demo The application includes a guided three-minute **Run Judge Demo** experience. The demonstration follows this story: 1. Project Orion begins with a health score of 82. 2. A new meeting update is received. 3. ProgramPilot extracts multiple weak signals. 4. Signal Correlation identifies a critical dependency. 5. A Release 3 warning is generated. 6. Program health falls from 82 to 68. 7. The application explains the 14-point change. 8. The Decision Assistant recommends an escalation. 9. An executive steering update is generated. 10. The potential business impact is quantified. --- ## Demonstration Scenario The application uses realistic sample data for **Project Orion**, an enterprise CRM-transformation program. The primary scenario involves: - Delayed vendor security approval - Blocked integration testing - Missing contingency ownership - Engineering capacity exceeding 110% - A predicted nine-day delay - Estimated cost exposure of $420,000 All financial and time-based values are prototype estimates created for demonstration purposes. --- ## Technology The GitHub Pages version is designed as a lightweight, browser-based application.
